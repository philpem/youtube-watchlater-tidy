I am particularly interested in:

- technically deep material rather than superficial summaries
- electronics, embedded systems and test equipment
- retrocomputing, restoration and unusual computer architectures
- communications engineering and technology history
- careful repair, reverse engineering and investigative work

I am less interested in:

- generic unboxing or reaction content with little original analysis
- clickbait whose useful information is very thin
- transient news whose value has expired

Do not penalise short comedy, cultural, or classic clips merely because they are not educational.
